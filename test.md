## Shyvana

**Stats**

* Base HP: 665 -> 625
* HP5: 8.5 -> 7
* Base AD: 66 -> 62
* Base AS: 0.658 -> 0.625
* AS Growth: 2.5% -> 2.0%
* Armor Growth: 4.55 -> 3.0
* MR Growth: 1.5 -> 1.25
* Attack Range: 125 -> 150
* Base MS: 350 -> 345
* Classification: Tank - Fighter/Mage -> Brawler - Fighter/Tank

**Eternals**

* Dragon Punches: Fury from Attacks on champions -> Fury from damaging enemy champions

**Abilities**

* Passive - Scalemail:
    * Takedowns on champions, large minions, and monsters earn Shyvana stacks of Scalemail, each stack of grants Shyvana permanent 0.5 Armor and 0.35 Magic Resist.
    * Large monsters/Siege minions grant 1 stack.
    * Champions and Epic monsters grant 3 stacks.

* Q Emberstrike:
    * Passive: Shyvana Attacks deals 1% (+0.0017% bonus AD) (+0.01% AP) max Health magic Damage On-Hit.
        * Deals 15 damage to monsters.
        * Maximum health does not affect structures and against non-champions has a minimum of 30 and maximum of 200.
    * Active: Shyvana's next Attack strikes in a cone around the target, dealing 5/15/25/35/45 (+110% AD) (+15% AP) physical damage. This Ability may be Recast after an Attack or a short delay within 4 seconds; the Recast strikes in a circle around the target.
    * Dragon Form: Shyvana gains an additional Recast, causing her to bite the target of her next Attack dealing 7.5/22.5/37.5/52.5/67.5 (+165% AD) (+22.5% AP) true damage.
        * Dragon Form's final Recast deals 150% base form damage (the value noted above).
        * Dragon Form's final Recast cannot critically strike.
    * Both recasts gain 50 bonus attack range.
    * Both recasts AOE radius is 275 units.

* W Inferno Aegis:
    * Active: Shyvana wraps herself in flames for 2.5 seconds, gaining 60/80/100/120/140 (+10% bonus Health) (+200% Scalemail Stacks) shield, increased by 18/24/30/36/42 (+3% bonus Health) (+60% Scalemail Stacks) per nearby enemy champion and 25% Move Speed, increased to 43.75% when moving towards enemies.
        * Bonus shield per enemy is equal to 30% of the original shield (the value noted above).
        * When moving towards enemies, the bonus MS is 175% stronger (the value noted above).
        * Enemy champs must be within 600 units of Shyvana to count.
    * When the effect ends, the shield is broken, or upon Recast the area around her detonates, dealing 80/100/120/140/160 (+40% bonus AD) (+20% AP) magic damage.
        * The the area is 350 units around Shyvana.
    * Dragon Form: If the detonation hits an enemy champion Shyvana restores from 100-250 (+10% bonus AD) (+5% AP) to 200-500 (+20% bonus AD) (+10% AP) Health based on her missing Health.
        * The healing scales up to 200% total healing (the value noted above).

* E Molten Burst:
    * Shyvana launches a fireball at the target location dealing 80/120/160/200/240 (+70% AP) magic damage and Slowing by 80% for 0.75 seconds. It explodes upon striking an enemy or reaching its terminus.
        * Explosion radius is 150 units.
    * Dragon Form Shyvana's fireball is larger and pierces through enemies. Upon striking a champion, large monster, or reaching its terminus it triggers a pulse of fire that deals 100/150/200/250/300 (+87.5% AP) magic damage and Slows by 40% for 0.75 seconds. The fireball leaves behind a trail for 2 seconds that deals 15/20/25 (Level 1/11/16) (+5% AP) magic damage per second.
        * Overlapping Dragon Form explosions deal 50% damage.
        * Cast range is 40% larger in Dragon Form.
        * Dragon Form Fireball damage deals 125% base form damage (the value noted above).
        * Dragon Form Slow is equal to 50% of base form Slow (the value noted above).
        * Explosion radius is 300 units. Trail length is 100 units.

* R:
    * Passive: Shyvana generates Dragon Fury by striking enemies with Attacks and Abilities, increased while in Dragon Form and reduced when striking in an area against non-champions.
        * Shyvana gains 1.15 Fury per successful Attack/Ability. She gains 10% of this when striking non-champions.
        * Shyvana loses 6.67 Fury per second while in Dragon Form, but gains 200% bonus Fury generation.
        * NOT LISTED: Shyvana gains 0.25/0.75/1.25 passive Fury generation per R rank.
        * Shyvana gains 0.01 passive Fury generation per 1 Ultimate Ability Haste.
    * Active: Shyvana transforms into her Dragon Form, becoming Unstoppable, and flies to the target location breathing fire on enemies below dealing 150/250/300 (+100% AP) magic damage and causing them Flee for 0.75 seconds.
        * Shyvana must travel a minimum of 650 units, up to 1050 units.
    * While transformed, Shyvana's basic Abilities are upgraded, she gains 100/200/300 (+300% Scalemail Stacks) bonus Health, 5/12.5/20% bonus Size, and 20/50/75 bonus Attack Range. Shyvana loses Dragon Fury over time and her transformation ends when she has no Dragon Fury remaining.
