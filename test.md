## ARAM: Mayhem

### Augments

**Fortified (Silver)**

* Gain 20 Armor, then increase your bonus Armor by 20%.

**Mystic Guard (Silver)**

* Gain 20 Magic Resist, then increase your bonus Magic Resist by 25%.

**Slime Time (Silver)**

* Every 6 seconds Autocast a burst of slime from your body, dealing 40-80 plus 3-6% (+1.5% per 100 AP) (+1.5% per 100 bonus AD) max Health magic damage to nearby enemies. If you hit a champion, secrete a blob nearby. Walking over the blob restores 3-7% max Health, while enemies destroy the blob.

**Steadfast Presence (Gold)**

* Gain 16% bonus Armor, Magic Resist and 20% Tenacity. Increased to 32% Armor, Magic Resist and 40% Tenacity while below 40% maximum Health.
