## ARAM: Mayhem

### Augments

**Critical Rhythm**

* Getting a IDCD (per spell ID, aka DOTs) of 4 seconds.
* Augment should now work with Jeweled Gauntlet and Vulnerability crits.
  * Thanks R0gueFool for the clarification.

**Dimension Shift**

* Bonus AD: 25-100 -> 35-150
* Bonus AP: 25-100 -> 35-150
* Bonus Health: 200-1000 -> 300-1500
* Cooldown: 180 seconds -> 128 seconds

**Double Tap**

* Getting a IDCD of 4 seconds.
* Augment should now work with Jeweled Gauntlet and Vulnerability crits.

**DropBear**

* Tibbers Base Health: 1500-6000 -> 1500-4500

**Dropkick**

* Execute Threshold: 5% (+3.5% per 100 base AD) (+0.2% per 100 bonus Health) -> 4% (+5.5% per 100 base AD) (+0.1% per 100 bonus Health)

**High Roller**

* Anvil drop method changed. Unable to gleam any details.

**Ok Boomerang**

* Cooldown: 10 seconds -> 7 seconds

**Poro Stampede**

* Snack Spawn Timer: 35-20 seconds -> 25-15 seconds
* Damage: 50-100 (+20 per Poro Love stack) -> 100-200 (+20 per Poro Love stack)
* Cooldown: 120 seconds -> 105 seconds

**Snowblast**

* Recast Damage: 100-275 (+40% bonus AD) (+20% AP) magic damage -> 100% bonus damage

**Squishy Slappy Grab**

* Shielding per Enemy pulled: 25-75 (+50% bonus Armor) (+50% bonus MR) -> 35-150 (+50% bonus Armor) (+50% bonus MR)

**Tooth Fairy**

* Lethality per Tooth: 4 -> 3
  * Magic Pen unchanged

### Champions

* Q now counts as a "low damage" spell.
