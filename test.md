**Mel**

* Base AD: 54 -> 52
* AS Growth: 2.5 -> 2.75
* Armor Growth: 5.2 -> 5.4
* Base Mana: 480 -> 455
* Mana Growth: 28 -> 38

* P:
  * Base Damage: 8-30 -> 4-30
  * Minion Mod: 50% -> 55%
  * Now seems to effect/be consumed on enemy wards?
    * PBE was not playable at the time of me writing this.

* Q:
  * Mel unleashes a barrage of 6-10 missiles that explode at the target location.
  * The first time an enemy is struck, they take 60 (+25% AP) magic damage, with subsequent hits dealing 5-25 (+8% AP) magic damage each.
  * If the volley hits an enemy champion at least once, this ability's cooldown is reduced by 0.5-1.0 (interp) seconds.
  * Mana Cost: 70-90 -> 65-80
    * Explosion Radius: 225 -> 200
    * Missile Speed: 3800 -> 3200
    * Interaction with Mordekaiser R adjusted.
    * Now has a 0.75 second Queue Threshold.
    * Cast type changed to match abilities like Aphelios Turret, Seraphine Q, Hwei Circle-abilities.

* W:
  * Mel unleashes a refraction nova that destroys enemy projectiles it collides with, within 400 units.
  * Mel retaliates for 35-55 (+10% AP) magic damage. Subsequent retaliations against the same enemy deal 5-25 (+5% AP) magic damage instead.
  * The nova also grants 50-170 (+50% AP) Shield and 10-30% (+4% per 100 AP) Move Speed for 2s to Mel and her allies. 
  * Cannot retaliate more than 7 times against each individual enemy
  * Cooldown: 38-26 seconds -> 30-24 seconds.
    * Note: Mode-specific cooldowns are currently unchanged.
  * Mana Cost: 80-40 -> 100-50
    * No longer has a cast time.
    * No longer counts as "Immune" for the purposes of augments.
    * Now counts as a "Boon" for the purposes of augments.

* E:
  * Root Duration: 1.5 seconds -> 0.8-1.0 seconds, scaling up to 1.6-2.2 seconds based on travel time.
  * Slow Amount: 30% -> 20-40%
  * Mana Cost: 50-90 -> 40-80

* R:
  * Cooldown: 120/100/80 seconds -> 120/90/60 seconds

**One Hit Wonder (Gold)**

**RISE (Prismatic)**

* If you use [SpellName] to kill an enemy champion, you may Recast it once within 5 seconds. Each Recast deals additional subsequent damage.
