## Voice Chat

* Voice chat has now started PBE testing!
  * It was not actually working though, in the 2 games I checked.
* Voice chat masking is powered by Voicemod.
  * Currently only changeable in the client, not in game.
  * Toggleable to be heard by both party and team, or just team
* Includes 5 voice banks:
  * Note: There is no official "guide" to the voice sounds yet. Here are my results from testing, as a trans woman with a deeper voice, sitting around the C2-C3 range.
  * Markus - A baritone voice. Very Draven-like.
  * Bexter - A tenor voice. Similar to Rakan.
  * Tarys - An alto voice. Similar to Senna.
  * Raghul - A very bass voice. Mordekaiser-like? Or Rhaast down an octave.
  * Jayra - Somewhere between alto and contralto. Between Bexter and Tarys but leaning more femme.

## ARAM: Mayhem

### Bands

**Bit Rush**

* Members: Arcade Miss Fortune, Corki, Riven, Ahri, Hecarim
* Effect: Collect Tokens by getting Takedowns. Cash in 4 Tokens to revive while dead, granting 125% MS for 10 seconds.

**Debonair Zed**

* Members: Debonair Zed
* Effect: Augments grant additional Gold and Hats!
  * Gold values are 200, 350, 500, 750.

**La Ilusión**

* Members: La Ilusión Qiyana, Draven, Renata
* Effect: Allies drop a candle on death. Pick it up to shield yourself and nearby allies.
  * Shield: 150 (+15% max Health) OR 250 (+35% max Health)
  * Shield targets allies within 1500 units and lasts for 4 seconds.
  * Candles last 10 seconds.

**Maestro Jhin**

* Members: Maestro Jhin
* Effect: Gain an additional Augment from a selection of 4.
