## ARAM: Mayhem

### Augments

**From Downtown**

* Range: 700 units -> 600 units

**Nature is Healing**

* 0.6-2.1% Health per second -> 15% Health per second
* Bushes now have individual cooldowns of 20 seconds.

**Nightstalking**

* Additionally grants 40% Move Speed on takedown.

**Skilled Sniper**

* Range: 700 units -> 600 units

**Soul Eater**

* Now grants 300 bonus Health if taken as second Augment or later.

**Upgrade Ravenous Hydra (Gold)**

* Your spells now trigger Cleave against enemy champions. Your Ravenous Hydra gains an additional 18% Lifesteal. Gain 500 Gold.
  * Cooldown of 1 second.
* NOTE: Classic-ish is not receiving this augment.
  
**Upgrade Sundered Sky**

* Reduce Sundered Sky's per target cooldown to 5 seconds. Your Sundered Sky's empowered attacks heal you for an additional 9% missing Health. Gain 500 Gold.
* NOTE: Classic-ish is not receiving this augment.
