**Clown College (Prismatic)**

* Re-enabled
* Backstab: You deal 20-35 (+50% bonus AD) (+35% AP) bonus physical damage when striking from behind.
* Deceive (Summoner Spell): You teleport and become Invisible for 3 seconds. Your next Attack while Invisible deals an additional 100 (+150% bonus AD) (+55% AP) physical damage. If striking from behind, this Attack critically strikes for 155% (+100% bonus Crit Damage) damage.
* Hallucinate: Death: On death, deal @FearDamage*100@% maximum Health magic damage and fear for 1 second around you.

**Droppybara (Prismatic)**

* Re-enabled
* Summoner Spell: After 2 seconds, a Capybara lands on the ground, dealing 25% max Health true damage.

**Don't Stop Cleavin' (Gold)**

* You may purchase unlimited amounts of The Black Cleaver. On purchase, increase the new Black Cleaver's stats by 25% for each copy you already have. Instantly gain The Brutalizer.

**Upgrade: Zz'Rot Portal (Silver)**

* Zz'Rot Portal spawns 1 additional minion, plus an additional minion for every 6 levels you have.
  * Minions are also spawned when the portal is destroyed.

**Do or Die (Prismatic)**

* Double the max stacks of Mejai's Soulstealer, Leviathan, and Sword of the Occult, but lose twice as many stacks on death.

**Drink Up (Silver)**

* Everyone on your team instantly gain 13 Health Potions.
* Gain 5 Health Potions every 5 takedowns.

**Time Warp: Heartsteel (Gold)**

* After gaining 600 Bonus Health, reach into the future and gain a Heartsteel. 
* Heartsteel is cannot be sold.

**Haste Makes Waste (Prismatic)**

* Gain 5% Cooldown Reduction. Increase your Cooldown Reduction cap by 20%.

**Stabby Stabby (Silver)**

* Gain 0.375% Cooldown Reduction per 1 Lethality. Increase your Cooldown Reduction Cap by 5%

**Upgrade: Deathfire Grasp (Gold)**

* Increase Deathfire Grasp's damage amp to 50% and reduce its Cooldown by 50%. Deathfire Grasp now targets all enemy champions within a 800 unit radius of your target.

**Upgrade: Innervating Locket (Gold)**

* Increase Innervating Locket's Health and Mana restored by 200%. Innervating Locket restores Health and Mana to all allied champions within 800 units around your target

**Doran's Biggest Fan (Prismatic)**

* Each purchase of Doran's Blade, Ring, and Shield beyond the first grants a stack to the original item. Each stack grants 100% of the item's stats.
  * The passive effects of these items do not increase.
  * Your respective Doran's items are no longer sellable.

**True Eternity (Prismatic)**

* You may purchase unlimited Rod of Ages. Your Rod of Ages' stack faster, and their stacks are unlimited. Gain a Catalyst the Protector.

**The Hex Core (Prismatic)**

* Get a Prototype Hex Core. Spend gold to upgrade the Hex Core and permanently upgrade your abilities.
  * Appears to be 20% increased Healing/Shielding/Damage per upgrade per ability.

**Spirit of the Jungle Main (Gold)**

* Your jungle item's primary stats are increased by 200%.

**Ragebait (Silver)**

* Gain 10% Dodge Chance.

**Bounce of the Poro King (Prismatic)**

* Re-enabled
* Bounce of the Poro King (Summoner Spell): For 3 seconds, transform into the Poro King! You are now Unstoppable and Ghosted. Gain 70% damage reduction and 40% Movement Speed, but become Silenced and unable to Attack.
* Additionally, bounce every 1 second, dealing 100-700 physical damage and Knock Back nearby enemy champions on landing.
  * The Poro King cannot Knock Back the same enemy more than once per cast.

**Grandma's Chili Oil (Gold)**

* Re-enabled
* Basic attacks and ability hits against enemies inflicts them with a Burn that deals 100-350 magic damage for 3 seconds. This effect has a 5 second cooldown per spell instance. Applying a Burn drops a Chili Oil pool nearby for 5 seconds. 
* Chili Oil pools restore 10-50 (+25 per Burn source, up to 250) Health and Burn enemies for 150 (+50 per Burn source, up to 500) magic damage per second.

**Laser Heal (Prismatic)**

* Re-enabled
* Cast a laser for 2.5 seconds that heals allies for 200-550 (+70% AP) (+25% bonus Health) health, and deals 50-300 (+50% AP) magic damage to enemies and Slows them for 20%.

**Snowball Roulette (Gold)**

* Gain 100 Ability Haste on Snowball. Hitting an enemy casts a random beneficial Summoner Spell on you and a harmful one on them. Following up recast the beneficial one on yourself. If you don't have one, gain a Snowball

**Poro Blaster (Gold)**

* Re-enabled
* You gather Poros every 5 seconds. Damaging an enemy fires all Poros at them, each dealing target's 3% max Health true damage. If you have 5 Poros, they will Knock Back once.

**I'm a Baby Kitty Where is Mama (Prismatic)**

* Re-enabled
* Standing next to an ally marks them as your mama. Gain 37.5% Movement Speed when moving towards Mama. Gain 75% Movement Speed and 15-50% Heal and Shield Power when close to your mama.

**??? (Prismatic)**

* Re-enabled
* Your Missing Pings will launch a missile at the pinged location that deals 100-400 + 5% missing Health magic damage to enemies and heals allies for 50-200 + 5% missing Health.
