Just a reminder that PBE changes are not final and subject to change. Some changes, such as bugfixes, may not be able to be viewed. 

## Champions

**Mordekaiser**

* R no longer counts as an immobilizing spell.
  * This means it no longer counts towards Imperial Mandate or (likely) the Mercy Augment in Arena.

**Ornn**

* Q now counts as an immobilizing spell.
  * This means it now counts towards Imperial Mandate and (likely) the Mercy Augment in Arena.

**Viego**

* R now counts as an immobilizing spell.

**Yorick**

* W now counts as an immobilizing spell.

## Items

**Black Cleaver**

* Cooldown per target: 0.2 seconds -> 0.01 seconds
* Now applies on all physical damage again, such as runes or item damage.

**Bloodletter's Curse**

* Cooldown per cast instance: 0.2 seconds -> 0.3 seconds

**Imperial Mandate**

* Effect properly extends the duration rather than stacks it.

## Runes

**Aftershock**

* Bonus Armor/MR Ratio: 80% -> 75%

**Deathfire Touch**

* Damage type: Adaptive damage -> Magic Damage

## Systems

**Touch of the Void (Void Grubs Buff)**

* Burn Damage per Second: 6/18/24 (3/9/12) -> 8/24/32 (4/12/16)
  * Total Damage: 24/72/96 (12/36/48) -> 32/96/128 (16/48/64)

### Systems

**Combo Break**
